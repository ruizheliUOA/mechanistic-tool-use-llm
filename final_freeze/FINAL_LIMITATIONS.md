| # | limitation | prevents claiming |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One formal ADMIT (Qwen3-8B); Qwen3-4B and Gemma returned FORMAL_DECLINE** on the destination-interval conditions despite positive point estimates (0.5781, 0.6296). The positive control was never run and its preregistration postdates outcome exposure. | that repair is *generally* licensable across models; any headline built on licence count. **Does not license** calling the declined models uncorrectable — the evidence is insufficient under this protocol and sample support, which is the gate behaving as designed |
| 2 | **Historical/modern protocol heterogeneity.** Phi, Qwen2.5-7B, Llama, Mistral predate the sealed protocol. | pooling historical and modern rows into one statistical comparison |
| 3 | **Dose and protocol dependence.** Exit-matched cross-model destination differences flip sign; target-hit varies 0.53→0.91 with dose and is non-monotone. | intrinsic model correctability; "model X is more correctable"; margin explains rung placement |
| 4 | **Phi execution variability.** Frozen 107/52 vs an independent run of the same code and seed giving 106/44, with routing 293 vs 243. Cause undetermined — the repository was squashed into one initial commit. | 52/93 as a stable constant; any exact-reproduction claim for the historical Phi pipeline |
| 5 | **Incomplete historical placebo destination logs.** Four of five E-2 arms are aggregate-only. | destination-resolved comparison of the historical control battery |
| 6 | **MetaTool binary-space degeneracy.** `OTHER_WRONG` empty by construction; `target_hit ≡ 1`. | any Correctable or destination claim on MetaTool |
| 7 | **Score-space near-equivalence.** 37 vs 38 gold arrivals; paired tests ns after Bonferroni. | that activation intervention is superior to a cheaper score-space intervention |
| 8 | **Representation-level, not circuit-level.** Multi-PC emergent; no mechanistic circuit isolated. | "we identify a causal circuit"; any single-axis semantic account |
| 9 | **Benchmark breadth.** Licensing rests on When2Call alone. | that the framework is validated across benchmarks |
| 10 | **Vacuous preservation at zero exposure.** Qwen3-4B `ca→direct` (exposure 0 at every dose); Qwen3-8B E2 (n=6, detectable only above 23.5%). | reporting 0% collateral as successful preservation in those settings |
| 11 | **E1/E2 gate asymmetry.** The frozen gate adjudicates preservation on E1; Qwen3-4B dev q=0.5 gives E1 1.3% (passes) vs E2 5.7% (would fail). | that certified preservation is exposure-conditional |
| 12 | **Gating helps in many evaluated settings, not all.** Gemma: +16 gated vs +17 ungated. | any claim that gating is necessary, universally or in a particular setting (S-23) |
| 13 | **Thresholds are conventions.** The 0.50 destination and 5% collateral criteria are prespecified conservative conventions, not calibrated optima; with three sealed outcomes the licence is an evidential rule, not a calibrated classifier. *(Moved from §6, 2026-09-11; wording from the branch README scope statement.)* | that the thresholds are optimal, or that the licence predicts correctability in new settings |
