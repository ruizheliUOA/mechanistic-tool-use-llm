# Final paper outline — 10 sections

1. **Introduction** — pre-execution tool-decision failure; the gap between
   behavioural improvement and verified correction; four contributions.
2. **Problem definition** — action space, directional error channels, what is
   excluded (arguments, execution, hallucination, binary tool/no-tool).
3. **SAKIKO: Discover, Detect, Correct** — the method. Cite CAST as closest prior
   art **here**, not defensively in Related Work.
4. **Can we correct tool-decision errors?** — Act 1, structural controls,
   cross-model correction evidence. Figures 2, Tables 1–2.
5. **Why aggregate gain is not enough** — the turning point. Phi preservation
   failure **and** the activation-vs-score-space destination comparison.
   **Figure 3.** This is the paper's centre of gravity.
6. **Verify and License** — destination resolution, preservation estimands
   (E1/E2), the rung hierarchy, frozen criteria, the exchange rate.
7. **Evidence across models and channels** — rung distribution, MetaTool breadth
   and bidirectionality, protocol conditionality. Figure 4, Table 3.
8. **Why the design works** — mechanism and design analysis: Readable ≠ Steerable,
   estimator adaptivity, site dependence, gating and exposure, dose. Figure 5.
9. **Related work and limitations** — CAST boundary; the twelve limitations;
   the reproducibility disclosure.
10. **Conclusion.**

**Section 5 is the paper.** Sections 1–4 exist to make it land; 6–8 exist to make
it actionable.
