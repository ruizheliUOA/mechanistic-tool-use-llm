# Meta-review

**Scores: A 5, B 5, C 6, D 4, E 5, F 6.** Mean 5.2. Borderline, no champion, one clear detractor.

## The decision

The paper's value does not rest on the ADMIT. It rests on the **without-SAKIKO counterfactual**:
four of eight settings would have been reported more favourably without the licence, three of
them as successes — and one of those three *was* published as a success by these authors, with
the disconfirming number sitting in the same file.

That is a demonstrated, quantified contribution to research practice. It is not a large one.

## What the audit found against the paper

1. **The incremental value is narrower than claimed.** `Net + specificity` already removes three
   of nine units. Destination accounting changed **no** decision beyond that on observed data.
   The unique contributions are the collateral estimand, non-vacuity, and the CI layer.
2. **The primary collateral endpoint has a construct problem.** Reviewer C is right. Gemma's
   frozen 1/223 = 0.45% passes while the causally exposed 1/11 = 9.1% exceeds the bound. The
   frozen denominator is 95% inert rows.
3. **The licence is not a utility rule.** It aligns with no moderate cost regime; the
   utility-positive-in-100%-of-grid unit (Gemma `ca→direct`) is refused.
4. **A gap exists:** a near-zero-yield intervention passes if it is precise when it acts.

## What survives

The framework localises failure at six distinguishable rungs across nine units; every one of
ten conditions guards a pathology, three uniquely; and the discipline is prospectively enforced
with hash-frozen one-shot execution. **That is a reporting-and-licensing standard with
demonstrated bite, not a correctability classifier.**

## Recommendation

**Weak accept, conditional on the claim being narrowed to Level 2** (see `FINAL_GO_NO_GO.md`):
a prespecified evidential standard for when behavioural movement may be called correction —
**not** a method paper, and **not** a claim to identify which errors are correctable.
